The filenames do not describe layout differences alone. DOCX text and
embedded-image inventories were compared, and relevant existing PDF
pages were inspected at the pinned baseline.

| Guide | Existing PDF | Findings to preserve or reconcile |

| `Simple_Classifications_-_1pg_each_-_resizes_to_show_MS_IDs` | 18 pages | Contains highlighted source/manuscript IDs; some captions still have question-mark placeholders. Last DOCX change: May 7, 2025. |

The c8 continuation says keyboard **B** in the historical source-ID guide
(PDF page 9), versus **C** in the fuller guide (PDF page 10). C agrees with
the current c8 definition; B is the multiple-binding-reuse key. Preserve
the historical page under its outdated-instructions warning. The complete
[guide review](GUIDE_CONSISTENCY_REVIEW_2026-11-08.md) records this and the
other differences rather than treating an appended explanation as a repair
for every older inconsistency.

David BLACK's revised plan is an in-document appendix in the fuller
illustrated guide only, a rename and every-page warning for the historical
source-ID guide, and consistency corrections in the smallest printable guide.
The Markdown helpers will be renamed together to distinguish MVP reuse from
reuse plus future phenomena. The proposed names have not yet been applied.
For keyboard discrepancies David directs use of `classifier.param`: for
example, current `gni` uses J. Documentation corrections do not change the
classifier's executable configuration. Historical copies under
`code_screenshots_and_text_snippets` remain historical artifacts.

No Word or PDF file has yet been edited, appended, or regenerated by the
